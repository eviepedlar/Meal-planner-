Greek Yogurt Overnight Oats
25g protein320 kcal
▾
Ingredients (makes 3 jars — Mon / Wed / Fri)
600g0% fat Greek yogurtprobiotic
120gRolled oatsprebiotic
3 tbspChia seedsfibre
180mlSkimmed or oat milk
3 tspHoney
1 tspVanilla extract
How to make
1
In each jar mix 200g yogurt, 40g oats, 1 tbsp chia seeds, 60ml milk, 1 tsp honey and a drop of vanilla.
2
Stir well, seal the jars and refrigerate overnight.
3
Keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days. Grab and go in the morning.

King Prawn & Vegetable Noodle Stir Fry
32g protein420 kcal
▾
Ingredients (1 serving)
200gKing prawns, raw
80g dryRice noodles
1 headPak choifibre
3Spring onions, sliced
2 tbspLow-sodium soy sauce
1 tspSesame oil
1 thumbFresh gingeranti-inflam
2 clovesGarlicprebiotic
How to make
1
Soak rice noodles in boiling water for 4–5 min, drain and set aside.
2
Heat sesame oil in a wok, fry garlic and ginger 30 sec, then add prawns and cook 2–3 min until pink.
3
Add pak choi and spring onions, toss 2 min. Add noodles and soy sauce, toss everything together and serve.
